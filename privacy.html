Arrow Resolve Original Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

Arrow Resolve Original is a mobile arrow puzzle game developed by Kygsz (Ebubekir Kaygısız). Contact us about privacy at skyg66210@gmail.com.

2. Data used to provide the game

  • On-device progress: completed and unlocked levels, current puzzle state, settings, game statistics, streaks and hint-related state can be stored locally on your device.
  • Optional Google account: if you sign in, authentication providers may process an account identifier, email, and basic profile information such as your name and avatar, as supplied by Google and necessary for sign-in.
  • Optional cloud sync: if enabled, an account identifier, level progress, game statistics, settings, hint-related records and synchronization metadata may be stored with Supabase to synchronize between devices.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device/app diagnostics, ad interaction information and other data described in its own policies. Ad preferences and legally required consent choices are respected through the app's consent flow, where applicable.
  • Purchases: optional ad-removal purchases are handled through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment-card information. Purchase tokens, transaction status and entitlement records may be processed to verify and restore purchases.
  • Notifications: if available and enabled, reminders or streak notifications may rely on device notification permissions and locally stored scheduling information.

3. How data is used

We use information to run the game, restore local or cloud progress, authenticate your account, display and measure advertising, provide optional rewarded ad benefits, verify purchases, prevent abuse, support users and maintain game reliability.

4. Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, information may be processed by Google (Sign-In, Google Play, AdMob) and Supabase (authentication and cloud storage). Their own privacy policies apply: Google and Supabase. We do not intentionally sell your personal information.

5. Storage, security and retention

Local game information remains on your device until cleared or removed by the applicable app/device process. Account-related cloud data is kept while needed to provide your account and sync features, or until deletion is requested, subject to necessary security, fraud-prevention and legal retention. We use access controls and encrypted network connections; no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and deletion

You may use available guest/local play features without connecting a Google account. You may sign out, change applicable ad consent preferences, clear local app data or request deletion of your account and related cloud data. Visit Delete Account & Data or email skyg66210@gmail.com. Removing the app does not necessarily delete account data stored in the cloud.

7. Children and changes

We do not intentionally seek unnecessary personal data from children. Any use involving children must comply with the age requirements and protections applicable to Google Play and local law. We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or legal requirements, with the updated date shown here.
